T06 AUTHORITY INTERPRETATIONS

1. Reclaim incorporation contact
   The authority defines attraction, accumulation, and incorporation but no separate Neutral pickup radius. Incorporation begins when the point-like Neutral center enters the authoritative actor silhouette radius derived from current Shell mass. A center-coincident cluster is always in the cone.

2. Ordinary merge survivor kinematics
   "smaller stableId absorbs" is implemented literally: the smaller-ID cluster retains its position and velocity, masses/provenance sum, and each countdown takes the maximum. This matches T05 emergency slot recovery and adds no invented impulse.

3. Integer 18-degree cone
   Runtime simulation remains integer-only. cos(18 degrees) is frozen as Q1.15 value 31164 and compared with squared dot products; no float or runtime trig is used.

4. HUD maturity ordering
   Tick order moves Transit before maturity. When TO_SHELL_TRANSIT completes, the module becomes INACTIVE and the maturity step on that same tick changes 720 to 719. DUE is reached after exactly 720 maturity steps.

5. Automatic Due repayment
   Reclaim occurs at tick step 15, after Due repayment at step 05. AdvanceHudLoanEarly therefore accepts only the newly reclaimed amount carried from the previous tick. DUE moves at most that carried amount Shell->Transit, so pre-existing Shell is not silently seized. Partial repayment remains DUE until the exact module mass is present in Transit.

6. Reallocation lock
   The runtime contract freezes 90 ticks but does not place the start edge. The lock begins when reverse Transit fully restores the module, preventing a new dismantle until the restored placement has remained stable for 90 subsequent ticks.
