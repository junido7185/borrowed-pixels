T05 AUTHORITY INTERPRETATIONS

1. PARTIAL CHARGE COOLDOWN
   Authority defines projectile formulas for every mass 8..48 but assigns cooldown only to the standard completed stages 8/16/32/48. ChargeSlot therefore preserves `stageMass`. Interception changes current mass and partial projectile formulas, but release retains the cooldown of the last fully reached standard stage. This avoids inventing a fifth cooldown curve or rewarding interception with a shorter undocumented cooldown.

2. ACTIVE CHARGE COLLIDER
   No separate offset coordinate is frozen for Charge. It stays attached to the actor position and uses the actor collision silhouette, allowing the documented same-time priority Charge before Shell without inventing a muzzle offset. Current charge mass still controls interception arithmetic and projectile radius after release.

3. SATURATED NEUTRAL ALLOCATION
   A T05 collision may require a Neutral slot before T06's normal Neutral phase exists. The allocation path implements only the authority-required recovery prerequisite: closest pair with sum <=256, distance tie by `(minStableId,maxStableId)`, smaller stable ID absorbs. T06 still owns per-tick damping, ordinary merge scheduling, and reclaim.

4. PROJECTILE BOUNDARY VELOCITY
   A boundary conversion places Neutral two pixels inside and applies the §32.6 20% reflection to the impacted normal component; tangent is preserved. At an exact corner both normal components reflect.
