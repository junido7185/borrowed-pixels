T08A AUTHORITY INTERPRETATION

Production spec 8.6 requires recovered ESCROW to open the PLAYER door faster,
but supplies no numeric door-rate table. The implementation uses the smallest
integer, monotonic realization: progress per tick = 1 + floor(playerEscrow/8),
with the runtime-contract 4500 ticks as the opening threshold. Thus zero
recovery opens at the timeout and each complete 8-TILE group accelerates it.

This affects no claim transfer, settlement range, movement maximum, encounter
duration, coordinate, save layout, or game-rule constant. It is covered by a
dedicated zero-versus-48 escrow test and is recorded rather than claimed as an
explicit numeric authority value.
